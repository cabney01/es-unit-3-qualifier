6.1 — Defining Biomes

Review climate versus weather, temperature and precipitation, climatographs, abiotic factors, and gross versus net primary productivity (NPP = GPP − producer respiration).

6.2 — Terrestrial Biomes

Review tropical rain forest, tropical dry forest, savanna, desert, temperate rain forest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate grassland, chaparral, boreal forest (taiga), and tundra. Know their climates, vegetation, and adaptations.

6.3 — Aquatic Ecosystems

Review salinity; photic, aphotic, and benthic zones; littoral and limnetic zones; wetlands; estuaries; plankton; coral reefs; and aquatic ecosystem services.

7.1 — Our Planet of Life

Review genetic, species, and ecosystem diversity; species richness and evenness; ecosystem services; and resilience.

7.2 — Extinction & Biodiversity Loss

Review extinction versus extirpation, background extinction, endangered versus threatened species, habitat loss and fragmentation, invasive species, overharvesting, poaching, and climate change.

7.3 — Protecting Biodiversity

Review conservation, biodiversity hotspots, endemic species, wildlife corridors, habitat restoration, captive breeding, protected areas, and conservation laws.
